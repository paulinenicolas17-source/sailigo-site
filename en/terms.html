1. Purpose

Sailigo is an app that connects people who offer places on board their boat with people who are looking to sail.

The app is limited to making these connections. It does not organise any trip, charter any vessel, sell any transport service or take part in any decision made on board.

2. Signing up

Signing up is reserved for adults. The information you provide must be accurate, in particular the information describing your sailing experience.

An account is personal. You are responsible for what is published from yours.

3. Free of charge

Posting a trip, sharing your availability, applying and exchanging messages are free. No fee is charged on any berth.

The app does not handle any money. Any sharing of costs is discussed and settled directly between the participants, outside the app, which takes no part in it and collects no payment.

This sharing must be limited to the actual costs of the trip — fuel, berthing fees, provisions — divided between the people on board, skipper included, with no profit whatsoever. A trip offered in exchange for payment is a professional activity subject to specific regulations: it is not allowed on the app.

4. Responsibility on board

This is the most important point in this document.

The skipper is solely responsible for their vessel, their crew and the conduct of the trip. It is up to them to make sure that their boat is seaworthy, that it carries the safety equipment required by the regulations for the area sailed, that they hold the necessary qualifications, and that their insurance covers the presence of people on board.

The crew member must find out about the conditions of the trip and how demanding it is, and report any relevant physical or medical limitation. It is up to them to check their own insurance cover.

Sailing involves risks, including serious injury and death. Everyone goes on board knowingly and under their own responsibility.

The app has no control over the condition of the vessels, the actual competence of the people, the validity of insurance policies or the decisions made at sea.

5. Behaviour

Exchanges must remain respectful. In particular, harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexual content, identity theft and any commercial use of the app not provided for are forbidden.

A report can be made from every profile. Blocking is immediate and requires no justification.

6. Published content

You remain the owner of the texts and photos you publish. You grant the app the right to display them to other users as part of its operation.

You guarantee that you hold the rights to what you publish, in particular to photographs showing other people.

7. Reviews

Reviews left after a trip only become visible once both people have given theirs. They must be honest and factual.

A review that is clearly defamatory or unrelated to the sailing concerned may be removed.

8. Reports, moderation and suspension

Every profile has a report button. Anyone, even without an account, can also report content they consider unlawful by writing to contact.sailigo@gmail.com, stating the content concerned and the reason for the report.

Every report is reviewed by a person, without any automated decision, normally within a week, and without delay when it concerns a threat, harassment or clearly unlawful content. Content that breaches these terms or the law may be removed, and an account suspended or deleted.

Any decision to remove content or suspend an account is explained and notified by email to the person concerned. They can challenge it by replying to that message, or by writing to contact.sailigo@gmail.com: their request will be reviewed again.

You can delete your account at any time, from your profile, under “Delete my account”, or by writing to us at contact.sailigo@gmail.com.
