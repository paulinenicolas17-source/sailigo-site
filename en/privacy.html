Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

About this translation

This English version is provided for your convenience. The French version of this document is the only one that is legally binding, and prevails in the event of any discrepancy.

Who processes your data

Pauline Nicolas, publisher of the Sailigo app, who can be reached at contact.sailigo@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account: email address and password, managed in encrypted form by our host — we have no access to it.

Your profile: display name, photos, biography, home port, level of experience, usual positions, qualifications, sailing CV.

Your activity: trips posted, applications, availability, reviews, reports, people followed or blocked.

Your exchanges: private messages and group messages.

Your location: only if you use the search around you. It is used to calculate a distance and is not stored.

Your place searches and the map display: when you type a harbour or look at the map, these requests are sent to the services that provide the address search and the base map, together with your device’s IP address.

Your device: a technical identifier if you accept notifications.

Why

To run the service: show trips, allow applications, deliver messages.

To keep people safe: mutual reviews, reports, blocking.

To keep you informed: notifications about your activity, if you have accepted them.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you signed up for, and our legitimate interest in protecting users.

Who has access

Other users can see your profile, your reviews and your posts: this information is public within the app, so do not put anything there you do not want to make visible. Your sailing CV is private by default: it is only visible to people you are in contact with, unless you choose to make it public.

Your private messages can only be read by the person you are talking to, and group messages by the members of the crew. The publisher never accesses them, except to handle a report that concerns them, and then only the messages involved. Your reports are only visible to the publisher.

The app relies on service providers, who only receive what they need to provide their service:

  • Supabase hosts the database, the accounts and the photos, on servers located in the European Union.
  • Resend sends the email address confirmation and password recovery emails: it receives your email address.
  • Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging, a Google service, deliver notifications: they receive your device’s technical identifier.
  • MapTiler provides the base map, using OpenStreetMap data: it receives your IP address and the area displayed.
  • Photon, a komoot service, provides the place search: it receives the text typed and your IP address.
  • Google Play and the App Store distribute the app, under their own terms.

Some of these providers are established outside the European Union, notably in the United States. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided for by the European data protection regulation, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

No data is sold or passed on for advertising purposes.

How long

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. An account that has not been logged into for three years is deleted, after a warning email sent one month beforehand.

When it is deleted, your profile, photos, trips, applications, messages, sailing CV and reviews — both written and received — are permanently erased. Only the reports and suggestions you have sent are kept, with no link to your identity, for the safety of other users.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data.

Most of this can be done directly in the app: edit your profile, delete your photos, remove an entry from your CV, or delete your account from your profile. For anything else, write to us at contact.sailigo@gmail.com.

You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority, if you believe your rights are not being respected.

Trackers

The app uses no advertising trackers and does not measure your audience for commercial purposes. The sailigo.org website sets no cookies.
