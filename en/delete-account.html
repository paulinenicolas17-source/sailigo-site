Delete your Sailigo account

Sailigo app, published by Pauline Nicolas.

From the app

This is the quickest way, and the deletion is immediate.

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. At the very bottom, tap Delete my account.
  3. Type the confirmation word shown on screen, then confirm.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled it, write to us from your account's email address. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm the deletion by return email.

Request deletion by email

Delete only some of your data

You do not have to delete your account to erase something. In the app, the following items can be deleted one by one, and disappear immediately:

  • A photo — Profile tab, tap the photo, then “Delete”.
  • A trip in your sailing CV — Profile tab, “My sailing CV”, then “Delete” on the trip concerned. Only the trips you entered yourself can be removed: those made through the app also belong to the skipper who confirmed them.
  • A qualification — same screen, “Remove” button.
  • A trip you posted — open it, then “Cancel the trip”. If nobody has signed up, it is deleted; otherwise it is cancelled and your crew is notified.
  • An availability — Crew tab, then “Remove”.
  • Your whole sailing CV — you can make it private rather than delete it: it then stays visible only to the people you are in contact with.

For any other partial deletion request, write to us at contact.sailigo@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, your profile and photos, the trips you posted, your applications and availability, your messages, your sailing CV and qualifications, the reviews you wrote and those about you, the people you follow and those you blocked.

What is kept

The reports and suggestions you sent remain, with no link to your identity, for the safety of other users. No other data is kept after deletion.
